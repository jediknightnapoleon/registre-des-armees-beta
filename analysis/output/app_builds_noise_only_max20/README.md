# Cost-effective builds (noise-only, ≤ 20 cards), ready to import into the app

Generated by `analysis/export_builds_for_app.py` from `analysis/cost_effective_builds.py` (method and caveats: `analysis/output/cost_effective_builds_noise_only_max20.md`). Four builds per army, one file each, in the app's saved-build format.

Unit values here are size-harmonised for noise only (report §5); the fully harmonised version is in `app_builds_max20/`. Build ids and names carry the version, so the sets can be imported side by side.

**Micro cap: at most 20 cards** including the staff general (19 units), set with `python analysis/cost_effective_builds.py --max-cards 20`. The game allows 31; fewer units are easier to control, which no price captures.

**To use one:** in the app, ⤓ Offline → *Import saves…* → pick the file, then open the build from your saved builds. *Save image* in the bottom tray renders its unit cards. For Theatre-of-War armies, *Generate times* finds a window in which the roll offers every unit.

- **Build 1** is the max-value build.
- **Build 2** is the balanced build (≥ 4 cavalry, ≥ 2 artillery) when it differs, otherwise the runner-up (the best build differing from build 1 in at least one unit).
- **Build 3** takes the army's highest-star staff general (cheapest on a tie) and the max-value build with the budget left. Its general is valued by the price rule alone, so its efficiency drops by whatever the better general costs beyond that value.
- **Build 4** draws its units *and* staff general from at most 4 source corps, which is all one Theatre-of-War roll offers without changing the clock (`NTW3AC.ToWFarmycorps`, max 4). The staff general is chosen jointly, since his corps uses one of the 4. Custom armies have no source corps, so their build 4 equals build 1. A combat general must still be among the 4 the roll draws from those corps.
- **Efficiency** = the build's normative value ÷ its cost; *for its rating* removes the corps-number effect.
- **Rules:** one staff general (valued by the global general price rule and chosen by the optimiser; build 3 takes the highest-star one) + up to 19 units = 20 cards (the user's micro cap; the game allows 31), at most one combat general, 10 000 funds, unit caps, artillery and heavy-cavalry caps. Files are numbered by build 1's efficiency.
