# Two-compartment builds (baseline)

Generated by `analysis/two_compartment.py`. Every card is scored on two compartments with equal weight: **A** = its efficiency (normative value ÷ cost) and **B** = its shrunk win rate in ladder games, each as a percentile rank within the army; total = A + B (0–2). A build maximises mean total score × 2·spent/10 000, the staff general counting as a card. Rules as in `cost_effective_builds.md`. † = over 240 models (normative value probably overstated).
