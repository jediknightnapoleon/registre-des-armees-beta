# Veteran's choice — what elite players' Army Corps builds say about card value (players weighted by AC win rate)

Generated by `analysis/veteran_choice.py fit` (method in its docstring). Aggregates only: the
row-level replay data (builds, player names) stays in `analysis/local/` and is not committed.

## Data

- **Builds:** 4800 Army Corps builds by 72 hand-picked elite players, in 215 corps; all legal at today's prices and rules. By year: 2023 15, 2024 170, 2025 1863, 2026 2752.
- **Comparisons:** 355450 legal one-card neighbours (swap, drop or add a card), commander variants and the commander slot kept fixed.
- **Weights:** no player counts for more than 40 builds; then × the player's AC win odds (Redcoatz results shrunk with 10 pseudo-games), scaled to mean 1 — per-player multipliers from 0.35 to 2.56 (effective builds 2652).
- **Sources:** 1163 of the builds are from Clan Games replays not on Redcoatz.

### What the net learned

- A linear fit of its log-multiplier on its inputs explains R² = 0.97 of it, so it is close to linear.
- Its log-multiplier correlates -0.92 with log(Quality value / list price): where the price model sees a bargain, the net shrinks it back toward the list price. The spread (sd) of log(value / price) falls from 0.286 (Quality) to 0.114; correlation of log value with log price rises from 0.890 to 0.985.
- The one-parameter blend measures the same thing directly: value = Quality^(1−a) · price^a with a = 0.87.
